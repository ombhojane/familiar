# Loop kinds and what "prepared" means for each

| Kind | Prepared means | Never do |
|---|---|---|
| **form** | Every field you can determine is filled; the remainder is listed with exactly what each needs | Submit it |
| **reply** | A complete draft in the person's voice, ready to send unedited | Send it |
| **intention** | Broken into the first concrete step, with anything needed to take it | Book or buy anything |
| **renewal** | Cost, deadline and exact renewal route confirmed | Pay |
| **promise** | The deliverable drafted, and the person it was made to identified | Deliver it |

## Judging what is genuinely missing

Something is missing only if the person alone can supply it: a decision, a private fact not in
the dossier, or a signature. If the dossier has it, or the screen shows it, it is not missing —
fill it in.

## Voice

Match the register visible in the capture. A grant portal is not a text message. When drafting
in the person's voice, use `dossier_read` for their writing style before inventing one.
